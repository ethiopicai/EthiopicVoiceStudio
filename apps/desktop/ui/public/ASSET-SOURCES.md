# Bundled assets

- `fonts/NotoSansEthiopic.ttf`: Noto Sans Ethiopic variable font, downloaded from the Google Fonts `ofl/notosansethiopic` directory. Redistribution license is included as `fonts/OFL.txt`.
- `ethiopic-ai-logo.png`: official EthiopicAI logo from https://ethiopic.ai/assets/ethiopic_ai_logo.png. EthiopicAI brand asset; trademark rights are reserved.

Assets are bundled locally. No external font or image requests occur at runtime.

- `profile-images/default-male.jpg`, `profile-images/default-female.jpg`, and `profile-images/presets/`: AI-generated fictional profile portraits for offline use. They do not depict or identify the source speakers.
- `reference-voices/preview-v3/`: synthetic noncommercial preview recordings. Source attribution and license terms are included in that directory's `LICENSES.txt` and catalog.
